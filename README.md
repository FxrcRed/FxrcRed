## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm an architect trained at **Columbia GSAPP**, based in Shanghai.

Currently working around: **AI-native design workflows · Generative visualization · Web 3D**

The past three years went into public buildings and urban renewal — theaters, TOD hubs, headquarters towers, competition entries, some of which won. I care about how a building gets built, not only how it looks on paper.

More recently I've been putting AI inside the design loop: rendering, competition films, and small tools where a general model is too dumb and a real product is too much.
